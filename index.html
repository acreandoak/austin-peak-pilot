All lists froze at 3:30 p.m. CDT. The recent rules used the preceding hour. The longer-history rule used every available main-panel reading up to the same cutoff.

The score is the selected homes’ combined mean HVAC draw from 4:30 to 4:45 p.m., using measured condenser and fan circuits. Measured draw is the starting point for a future curtailment test. August 24 and 25 are same-clock follow-ups. ERCOT’s August peak was on August 23.

    Coverage, data windows and reproducibility

    Reproduce with python3 replay.py source.zip --verify. The full pinned source checksum and machine-readable results are in results.json.

    Keep the existing list. Test in shadow mode.

    Start with a utility that already runs a thermostat program. Compare its current list with appliance-based targeting across multiple new peak dates at the same fixed budget. Verify data delivery before the cutoff and check appliance estimates against independent circuit measurements.

    Advance to randomized outreach only if the shadow test clears a utility-agreed bar. Judge the pilot on verified incremental peak kW per invite and cost per verified kW, alongside enrollment, opt-outs, rebound and comfort.
